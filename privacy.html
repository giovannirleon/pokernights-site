Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Poker Nights Tracker is designed as a local-first home poker tracking app. It helps users track poker groups, players, buy-ins, rebuys, shared expenses, chip counts, cash payouts, and stats.

Data Stored by the App

The app may store information you enter, including player names, phone numbers, poker group details, game sessions, buy-ins, rebuys, shared expenses, chip counts, cash payouts, and player stats.

Contacts

The app can optionally access your iPhone contacts when you choose to link a player to a contact. Contact access is used to fill in player names, phone numbers, and contact photos. Contact linking is optional.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with the developer or third parties. Poker game data is stored locally on your device.

Backups

Because app data is stored locally, it may be included in your normal iPhone device backups depending on your device and iCloud backup settings.

Analytics and Tracking

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
