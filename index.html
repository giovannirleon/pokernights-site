Support

Poker Nights Tracker helps home poker groups track buy-ins, rebuys, shared expenses, chip counts, cash payouts, and lifetime stats.

Privacy

The app stores poker group and game data locally on your device. Optional contact linking is used only to fill player names, phone numbers, and contact photos.

Read the privacy policy

Common Help

  • Create a poker group for recurring games.
  • Add players once, then reuse them in future sessions.
  • Use final settlement to calculate clear cash payouts.

Data Backup

Poker Nights Tracker saves data in the app's local documents storage, so it can be included in normal iPhone device backups.
